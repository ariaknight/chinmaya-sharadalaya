Discover the transformative power of yoga and meditation! Our classes blend traditional asana (poses) with pranayama (breath work) and meditation to promote physical health, mental clarity, and spiritual well-being.

**Class Structure:**
- Gentle warm-up and breathwork (10 min)
- Asana practice tailored to all levels (35 min)
- Relaxation and meditation (15 min)

**Benefits:**
- Improved flexibility and strength
- Better stress management
- Enhanced mental focus
- Deeper spiritual connection
- Better sleep quality

**What to Bring:**
- Yoga mat
- Water bottle
- Comfortable clothing
- Optional blocks and straps

**All Ages Welcome** - Modifications provided for beginners and advanced practitioners alike.
