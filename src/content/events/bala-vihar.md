Join us for Bala Vihar, our youth program designed to instill spiritual values and cultural knowledge in the next generation. Through engaging activities, storytelling, and Sanskrit lessons, children learn about Hindu philosophy and ethics in a fun, supportive environment.

**Program Highlights:**
- Sanskrit language basics
- Hindu scriptures and stories
- Meditation and yoga for children
- Cultural celebrations and festivals
- Community service projects
