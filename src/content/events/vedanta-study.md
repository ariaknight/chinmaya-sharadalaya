Join our Vedanta Study Circle for an in-depth exploration of Hindu philosophy and sacred scriptures. This weekly gathering brings together sincere seekers interested in understanding the deeper truths of existence through the lens of Vedantic teachings.

**Topics Covered:**
- Upanishads and their core teachings
- Brahmasutra and philosophical foundations
- Bhagavad Gita wisdom in modern life
- Self-inquiry and meditation practices
- Practical applications of Vedantic wisdom

**Ideal For:**
- Those seeking spiritual growth
- Students of philosophy and eastern wisdom
- Anyone interested in self-discovery
- Meditation practitioners wanting deeper understanding

**Format:**
- Scripture reading and discussion
- Q&A session
- Guided meditation
- Optional one-on-one guidance
