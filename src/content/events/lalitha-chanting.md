Experience the profound spiritual practice of chanting the Lalitha Sahasranama, a sacred hymn containing 1,000 names of the Divine Mother. This practice is believed to invoke peace, prosperity, and spiritual awakening in the hearts of participants.

**What to Expect:**
- Guided chanting in Sanskrit with English translations
- Explanation of mantra meanings and significance
- Meditation following the chanting session
- Light refreshments and community gathering afterwards
- Suitable for all levels of experience

**Spiritual Benefits:**
- Enhanced concentration and mental clarity
- Stress relief and emotional healing
- Connection to divine consciousness
- Development of devotional practices
