Celebrate the joy of devotion through bhajan singing! Bhajans are devotional hymns that express love and reverence for the divine. No prior musical experience necessary—just bring an open heart and enthusiastic spirit.

**About Bhajans:**
- Traditional Sanskrit and Hindi devotional songs
- Stories of Hindu deities and spiritual wisdom
- Group harmony and collective spirituality
- Ancient musical traditions kept alive

**Session Includes:**
- Teaching of new bhajans (with phonetic spelling)
- Group singing practice
- Explanation of spiritual meanings
- Refreshments and community time
- Optional recordings available

**Why Join?**
- Experience the peace of collective singing
- Learn about Hindu spiritual traditions
- Meet like-minded spiritual seekers
- Boost mental and emotional well-being
- No musical training required

Perfect for families and individuals of all ages!
